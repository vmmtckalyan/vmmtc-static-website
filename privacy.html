Privacy Policy

How we collect, use and protect your information.

Last updated: 17 September 2026

Who We Are

This website is operated by Vernon Memorial Methodist Tamil Church, Kalyan (located opposite SBI Bank, Kalyan–Murbad Road, Kalyan West, Maharashtra).

If you have any questions about this policy, you can contact us using the details below.

What We Collect

  • Information you send us by email, phone or through the contact details on this website.
  • Information shared through Google Forms for our past events, trips and souvenir book, such as your name, contact number and address.
  • Your language preference, stored locally on your own device so the site remembers whether you read English or Tamil.
  • Anonymous technical data your browser sends automatically, such as which pages you visit.

How We Use It

We use the information you share with us to respond to your enquiries, keep you informed about church life, and administer events, trips and other activities.

Third-Party Services

Some parts of this website use third-party services, which have their own privacy policies and may process limited data such as your IP address:

  • Google Fonts — to load the fonts used on this site.
  • Google Maps — to show our church location.
  • YouTube — to play and link to videos of our services and events.
  • Google Forms — hosted our past event, trip and souvenir forms.
  • Facebook and Instagram — links to our church social media pages.
  • Vercel — used to host our invoice and membership login tool.

Children in Our Gallery

Our gallery may include photos of children at church events. We share such photos only with the permission of their families.

If you are a parent or guardian and would like your child’s photo removed, please contact us using the details below. We will act on your request promptly.

Your Rights

Under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 of India, you have the right to access, correct and erase the personal information we hold about you, and to withdraw consent where consent is the basis of processing.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the details below.

Cookies and Local Data

This website does not set tracking cookies. It stores only your language preference in your browser’s local storage.

Read our Cookie & Local Data policy for full details.
