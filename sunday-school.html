Children’s Ministry

Who we are

We believe every child is a precious part of God’s family and an important part of the Church today. Our Sunday School at Vernon Memorial Methodist Tamil Church, Kalyan, provides a welcoming and caring environment where children can discover God’s love, learn about Jesus, and grow in faith.

Through Bible stories, songs, prayer, activities, games and age-appropriate lessons, our teachers help children understand God’s Word in ways they can enjoy, remember and apply in their everyday lives. Our goal is not simply to teach children about the Bible, but to help them build a personal relationship with Jesus, develop a foundation of faith, and learn to love God and serve others.

As they learn, play, worship and pray together, children are encouraged to grow in confidence, kindness and faith — knowing that they have a place in God’s Church and a part to play in His story.
