Men’s Fellowship

Who we are

The Methodist Men fellowship unites the men of VMMTC in faith, friendship and service. Together we build one another up through the Word, take up the practical work of the church, and model godly leadership at home and in the community.

Through prayer, Bible study, fellowship, worship and service, the fellowship encourages men to put their faith into action. Together, we support the life and ministry of the Church and respond to the needs of our community.

As brothers in Christ, we seek to grow in faith, serve with humility, lead by example, and be a positive influence in our families, Church and wider community.
